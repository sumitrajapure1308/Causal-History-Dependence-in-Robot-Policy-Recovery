# Causal-History Dependence in Robot Policy Recovery: A Matched-State Evaluation under Execution-Induced Failures

> **Status:** Phase 0 (Project Initialization & Audit Complete; Research Freeze Awaiting User Approval)  
> **Working Paper Title:** *Causal-History Dependence in Robot Policy Recovery: A Matched-State Evaluation under Execution-Induced Failures*  
> **Working Project Name:** `causal-history-recovery`

---

## 1. Project Identity and Principles

- **Independent New Research Project:** This is a completely new and independent robotics research project. It is **not** an extension of, merge with, or derivative of MIND-Contact or MIND-Contact++.
- **Core Investigation:** The project investigates **causal-history dependence in robot policy recovery**. Specifically, it tests whether robot recovery depends solely on the current physical state or also on the causal execution history that produced that state.
- **Core Methodology:** The primary method is **matched-state counterfactual evaluation** under different execution histories, comparing:
  1. **Condition A (Natural Policy-Induced Failure):** Genuine execution deviation generated by closed-loop policy rollout.
  2. **Condition B (Prefix-Replay Failure):** Exact replay of the initial state and action prefix leading to the deviation, preserving causal action history.
  3. **Condition C (Matched-State Injection):** Deterministic reset/injection into a physically matched state ($\epsilon$-bounded on generalized coordinates, velocities, and contacts) without the causal action prefix.
  4. **Condition D (Observation-History Masking / Ablation):** Explicit evaluation of policy recovery under varying history windows (current-only vs. short vs. full history).
- **Simulation Bounding:** All initial results are simulation-based (MuJoCo / PyBullet) and represent rigorously controlled counterfactual experiments. Sim-to-real transfer is explicitly treated as future work; no hardware claims are made without physical robot validation.
- **Scientific Integrity:** **No results are valid until generated by executable, verifiable code and experiments.** No fake metrics, fabricated citations, or premature novelty claims are permitted.

## 2. Research Questions and Core Hypotheses

### Primary Research Question
> *When physically equivalent robot failure states are reached through different execution histories, do robot policies show different detection, diagnosis, recovery, and final task-completion performance?*

### Formal Non-Equivalence
$$\text{same physical recovery state } s \not\Rightarrow \text{same recovery capability } R$$

### Core Hypotheses
- **$H1$ (History Dependence):** $H_0: R_{\text{replay}} = R_{\text{injected}}$ vs. $H_1: R_{\text{replay}} \neq R_{\text{injected}}$.
- **$H2$ (Benchmark Validity):** Policy rankings are altered when evaluated under causal-history protocol vs. conventional fixed-state or prefix-replay benchmarks.
- **$H3$ (Temporal Information):** History-aware policies exhibit distinct recovery and diagnosis profiles compared to memoryless reactive policies under matched states.
- **$H4$ (Recovery Training):** Policies trained with recovery augmentations exhibit lower history-dependence variance across matched failure states.

## 4. Execution Phases

- **Phase 0:** Project Initialization, Methodology Audit, Literature Audit, Research Freeze *(Current)*
- **Phase 1:** Simulator & Environment Validation (Deterministic reset, state serialization, prefix replay)
- **Phase 2:** Single Task & Baseline Policy Implementation
- **Phase 3:** Trajectory Collection & Failure Detection
- **Phase 4:** Simulator-State Saving, Restoration, and Action-Prefix Replay
- **Phase 5:** Matched-State Construction & Validation ($\epsilon$-thresholds)
- **Phase 6:** Recovery Evaluation across Conditions A, B, C, D
- **Phase 7:** Metrics and Statistical Analysis ($H_R$, Wilcoxon signed-rank, bootstrap CI)
- **Phase 8:** Ablation Studies (History window, matching threshold, recovery training)
- **Phase 9:** Multi-Task and Multi-Policy Generalization
- **Phase 10:** Paper Preparation and Public Reproducibility Package
