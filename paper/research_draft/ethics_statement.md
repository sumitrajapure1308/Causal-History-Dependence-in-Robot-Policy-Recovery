## 2. Ethical Declarations

### A. Human Participants & Animal Research Oversight
- **Human Participants:** This study involves solely physics simulation experiments and algorithmic benchmarking. No human participants, human clinical trials, or user surveys were conducted. Institutional Review Board (IRB) oversight or ethics clearance was not required.
- **Animal Subjects:** No animals or biological tissue were involved in any phase of this research. Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC) approval was not required.

### B. Physical Hardware and Live Field Experiments
- **Physical Robot Validation:** In accordance with explicit methodology bounding, no physical hardware experiments, real-world teleoperation trials, or physical robot testbed evaluations were conducted.
- **Physical Safety Hazards:** All failures and recovery trajectories were simulated in the MuJoCo physics engine; no physical safety risks to personnel or infrastructure were incurred.

### C. Responsible AI, Dual-Use, and Safety Implications
- The primary focus of this research is developing rigorous counterfactual evaluation protocols for failure recovery in robotic manipulation (planar pushing and sliding).
- The methodology and findings are intended to enhance the reliability, transparency, and safety of autonomous robotic manipulators operating in manufacturing, logistics, and service environments.
- No military, surveillance, or dual-use weaponized technologies were developed or investigated.
