1. **Author order:** The definitive numerical sequence of authorship (First author, second author, third author, etc.).
2. **Full legal name:** Full legal name as it should appear in the published IEEE Xplore record and citation indices (First / Given Name, Middle Name or Initial, Family / Last Name).
3. **Affiliation:** Official institutional or university name.
4. **Department or group:** Specific department, school, division, or research laboratory within the institution.
5. **City, state/province, country, postal code:** Complete institutional physical/postal mailing address.
6. **Institutional email address:** Official institutional/university email address for each author.
7. **ORCID iD:** Authenticated 16-digit ORCID identifier (`0000-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) registered and linked by each author.
8. **Corresponding author designation:** Clear specification of which author is designated as the primary corresponding author for IEEE editorial communication.

### Manuscript-Level Statements & Declarations:
