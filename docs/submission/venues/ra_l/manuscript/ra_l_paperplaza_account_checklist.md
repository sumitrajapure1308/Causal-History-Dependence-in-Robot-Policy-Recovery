# IEEE RA-L PaperPlaza Account and PIN Verification Checklist

**Target Venue:** IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L)  
**Portal:** PaperPlaza Conference and Journal Management System  
**URL:** `https://ras.papercept.net/journals/ral`  
**Purpose:** Actionable checklist for human co-authors to establish and verify their PaperPlaza PINs prior to live submission.  
**Strict Policy Directive:** Autonomous agents must NOT create PaperPlaza accounts, enter credentials, or upload files externally. This checklist is for human author action only.  
**Status:** `planned` (Author Action Required)  
**Date:** 2026-11-10  

---

## 1. PaperPlaza Account Overview

The IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) uses PaperPlaza (`ras.papercept.net`) for managing initial letter submissions, revisions, and reviews. Every contributing author must possess a unique Personal Identification Number (PIN). When the corresponding author submits the manuscript metadata, PaperPlaza queries the PIN for each co-author to automatically populate their profile, affiliations, and contact email.

## 2. Co-Author PIN Registration & Verification Checklist

Complete the following verification steps for each co-author before attempting live submission:

## 3. Subaccount Roles & Permissions on PaperPlaza

When entering the submission on PaperPlaza:
1. **Submitting Author:** Principal Investigator (Corresponding Author).
2. **Author Role Ordering:**
   - 1st: Lead Researcher (Author PIN #1)
   - 2nd: Systems Engineer (Author PIN #2)
   - 3rd: Robotics Lead (Author PIN #3)
   - 4th: Principal Investigator (Author PIN #4, designated as Corresponding Author)
3. **Automated Co-Author Confirmation:**
   Upon initial submission confirmation, PaperPlaza will automatically transmit automated email notifications to all 4 co-authors at their registered PIN email addresses.
