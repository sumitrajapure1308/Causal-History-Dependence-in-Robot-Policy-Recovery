## 1. PaperPlaza Account Policy and Regulatory Directives

All submissions to IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) are administered through the PaperPlaza Conference and Journal Management System (`https://ras.papercept.net`). IEEE and PaperPlaza regulations enforce:
1. **Mandatory Individual PIN Registration:** Every co-author must have a pre-registered PaperPlaza PIN containing their full legal name, institutional affiliation, country, and professional email address.
2. **Corresponding Author / Submitting PIN:** The submitting author must use their personal PIN and password to log in and register the initial submission. For this paper, Author 4 (Principal Investigator) is designated as the submitting contact author.
3. **Co-Author PIN Linking:** The submitting author adds each co-author to the submission record using their unique PaperPlaza PIN.
4. **No Automated Creation:** In adherence to project guidelines and ethics directives, no autonomous agent or AI tool may create accounts, simulate authentication, or enter credentials into external portals.

## 3. Human Operational Instructions for Portal Submission

When human authors decide to initiate external submission:
1. **PIN Registration / Retrieval:**
   - Navigate to `https://ras.papercept.net`.
   - Select **"PINs (Personal Identification Numbers)"** $\rightarrow$ **"Register a new PIN"** (or retrieve existing PIN).
   - Enter professional contact information matching the manuscript title page.
2. **ORCID Authentication:**
   - Within the PaperPlaza profile management menu, click **"Authenticate with ORCID"**.
   - Complete the OAuth 2.0 handshake to link your persistent 16-digit ORCID identifier.
3. **Paper Registration:**
   - Log into `https://ras.papercept.net/journals/ral`.
   - Click **"Submit a new manuscript"**.
   - Enter Title: `Causal-History Dependence in Robot Policy Recovery: A Matched-State Evaluation under Execution-Induced Failures`.
   - Enter Abstract: Paste the 170-word abstract verbatim from `ra_l_abstract.md`.
