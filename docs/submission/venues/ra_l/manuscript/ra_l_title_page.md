## 1. Official Single-Blind Review Protocol

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) operates under a **Single-Blind Peer Review** model:
- The reviewers remain anonymous to the authors.
- The authors' identities, institutional affiliations, postal addresses, and contact emails **MUST BE FULLY DISCLOSED** on the title page of the submitted manuscript.
