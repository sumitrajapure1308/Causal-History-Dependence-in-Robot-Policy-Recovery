## 1. Verified PaperPlaza Account Requirements

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) manages all submissions exclusively through the IEEE RAS PaperCept / PaperPlaza conference and journal management system:
- **PaperPlaza RA-L Submission Portal:** [`https://ras.papercept.net/journals/ral`](https://ras.papercept.net/journals/ral)

### Mandatory Author Account Prerequisites:
1. **PaperPlaza PIN (Personal Identification Number):**
   - Every listed author must possess an existing, active PaperPlaza PIN.
   - If an author does not have a PIN, they must register independently at PaperPlaza before paper registration.
   - Registration requires: Full legal name, institutional affiliation, department, complete physical postal address, country, and primary academic email.
2. **Submitting Author Privileges:**
   - The corresponding or submitting author must have active login access to submit the new manuscript via PaperPlaza.
3. **Strict Agent Constraint:**
   - **The autonomous AI assistant will NEVER create a PaperPlaza account, register a PIN, enter credentials, or log into the portal on behalf of authors.** Account creation and credential management remain strictly the responsibility of human authors.

## 2. Mandatory IEEE ORCID Requirement

IEEE mandates that all authors submitting manuscripts to IEEE journals must connect their Open Researcher and Contributor ID (ORCID) to their author profile:
- **ORCID Registration Portal:** [`https://orcid.org/register`](https://orcid.org/register)
- **Integration with PaperPlaza:**
  1. Author logs into PaperPlaza (`https://ras.papercept.net`).
  2. Selects *"My Profile"* or *"Personal data"*.
  3. Clicks *"Connect ORCID"* and authenticates through the official ORCID portal.
  4. PaperPlaza associates the verified ORCID iD with the author's PIN.
- **Verification Rule:** Submissions where author ORCIDs are missing or unlinked may be delayed or flagged during initial editorial screening.

## 3. Title Page Metadata Specification (Single-Blind Review)

Unlike double-blind venues, IEEE RA-L enforces a **single-blind review** protocol where reviewer identities are protected, but author identities must be fully disclosed on the title page:

### Required Elements on Page 1:
1. **Title:** Full title typeset in standard IEEE style.
2. **Author List:** Complete names of all contributing authors (e.g., *Firstname M. Lastname*).
3. **Affiliation Blocks:**
   - Department, laboratory, or research institute.
   - University, corporation, or organization name.
   - City, state/province, postal/ZIP code, and country.
4. **Email Addresses:** Official institutional email addresses for all authors.
5. **Corresponding Author Footnote:**
   - Explicit footnote: `*Corresponding author: Firstname Lastname (email@institution.edu)`.
6. **Manuscript Receipt & Funding Footnote:**
   - Title page footnote citing institutional support: `Manuscript received Month DD, YYYY. This work was supported by [Institutional Support / Unrestricted Research Grant].`
7. **Digital Object Identifier (DOI) Placeholder:**
   - Standard IEEE publication banner footnote: `Digital Object Identifier (DOI): see top of this page.`

## 4. User Provisioning Checklist Prior to External Submission

Prior to authorizing any external transmission, the user must provide:
- [ ] Confirmed list of authors in agreed authorship order.
- [ ] Registered PaperPlaza PIN for each co-author.
- [ ] Verified ORCID identifier for each co-author linked in PaperPlaza.
- [ ] Complete departmental and institutional postal addresses.
- [ ] Designated corresponding author email address.
- [ ] Explicit final authorization of the compiled IEEE two-column PDF.
