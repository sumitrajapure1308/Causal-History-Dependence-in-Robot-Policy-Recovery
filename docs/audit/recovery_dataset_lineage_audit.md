## 1. Scope of the Dataset Lineage Audit

To ensure the scientific integrity of the policies evaluated in Phases 7 and 8, this audit verifies the complete data generation and preprocessing pipeline for the recovery-augmented demonstration dataset (`data/processed/recovery_training_dataset.npz`). Specifically, we verify:
1. Episode-level train/validation splitting prior to sliding-window tokenization.
2. Complete absence of overlapping windows crossing split boundaries.
3. Complete separation between training seeds and benchmark/development scenario seeds.
4. Provenance and validity of expert action targets.
5. Strict unprivileged status of observation inputs to learned policies.

---

## 2. Train/Validation Split Verification

In Phase 3 nominal training (`train_policy.py`), an architectural defect was identified where all sliding windows across all episodes were shuffled together and split at the sample level ($80/20$). This caused adjacent windows ($t$ and $t+1$) from the same trajectory to appear simultaneously in training and validation sets.

### Phase 7 Correction Audit (`build_recovery_training_dataset.py`):
- **Source Episode Allocation:**
  - **Training Cohort:** 50 distinct episodes (25 nominal, 25 multi-phase recovery) collected across seeds **3000 to 3049**.
  - **Validation Cohort:** 10 distinct episodes (5 nominal, 5 multi-phase recovery) collected across seeds **3050 to 3059**.
- **Sliding Window Construction:**
  - Windowing was performed **strictly after episode partitioning**:
    ```python
    train_data = build_sliding_window_dataset(train_episodes, cfg["history_window"])
    val_data = build_sliding_window_dataset(val_episodes, cfg["history_window"])
    ```
  - **Result:** **0% overlap**. Exactly zero frames or windows from any training trajectory appear in the validation split.

## 4. Provenance of Expert Action Targets

The target actions in the dataset were generated by a scripted expert demonstration routine implementing a three-phase recovery strategy:
1. **Phase 1 (Retraction):** Robot end-effector executes a controlled backward motion ($a_x = -0.006\text{ m/step}$) for 8 steps to break dynamic jamming and disengage from the slipped cube.
2. **Phase 2 (Re-alignment):** End-effector navigates laterally to a standoff position ($4.5\text{ cm}$ behind the rotated cube) oriented along the vector pointing from the cube to the goal.
3. **Phase 3 (Directed Pushing):** End-effector closes the standoff gap and pushes forward along the goal direction.

### Verification of Failed vs. Successful Trajectories:
- All 50 training episodes and 10 validation episodes achieved task completion within the 120-step horizon (100% success rate).
- Failed recovery trajectories were **not** used as behavioral cloning targets. Only successful expert trajectories were ingested into `data/processed/recovery_training_dataset.npz`.

## 5. Input Feature Unprivileged Status

The observation inputs stored in the dataset were audited for potential privileged leakage:
- **Base Observation ($d=20$):** Consists exclusively of forward kinematics and object tracking observables available to standard vision/proprioception pipelines.
- **Causal Augmentation Features ($d=4$):**
  - Failure detection flag ($1.0$ if $|\text{yaw}| > 15^\circ$ or drift $> 2.5\text{ cm}$, else $0.0$).
  - Normalized deviation time $(t - t_{\text{dev}}) / 100$.
  - Instantaneous deviation yaw magnitude.
  - Normalized remaining horizon $(150 - t) / 150$.
- **Previous Action ($d=3$):** 1-step cached previous robot motor command.
- **Contact Indicator ($d=1$):** Binary touch sensor signal.

**Conclusion:** Zero privileged simulation state (e.g. contact normal forces, friction coefficients, unmodeled actuator dynamics, internal solver multipliers) was provided to the policy inputs. The dataset lineage is fully compliant with research standards.
